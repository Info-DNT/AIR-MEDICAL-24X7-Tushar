Air Ambulance From Davao: Complete Guide to Emergency Patient Transfers

November 20, 2026

Davao has major hospitals that can manage many medical conditions locally. Air transfers may be needed when a patient requires specialist care in another city or country, or when a patient needs to return home. Davao has major hospitals that can manage many medical conditions locally. 

This guide covers how those transfers are organised, including the part families ask about most, which is how a patient outside Davao City reaches the aircraft.

How Does an Air Ambulance Transfer From Davao Work?

An air ambulance transfer from Davao begins with a medical assessment and confirmation of a receiving hospital, followed by a ground ambulance from the ward to Francisco Bangoy International Airport and a medically equipped flight with a medical team on board. Transfers may be domestic, inter-island or international, depending on the patient's needs.

When a Patient in Mindanao Needs an Air Transfer

  1. Referral to a specialist centre for complex cardiac, neurosurgical, transplant or paediatric care.
  2. Patients in provinces where the nearest hospital cannot provide the level of care required.
  3. Foreign nationals who fall seriously ill or are injured while in the region.
  4. Returning OFWs who need a hospital bed near family in Mindanao.

Reaching Davao From Elsewhere in The Region

Patients often start somewhere other than Davao City: General Santos, Cotabato, Butuan, Tagum, or an island community. Road journeys across Mindanao can be long, and the right routing depends on the patient's condition, road and sea conditions, weather and what the nearest airport can accommodate.

The options generally considered are a ground ambulance to Davao followed by the flight, or an aircraft able to operate into a nearer regional airport, where one is available and suitable. Aircraft and airport suitability has to be confirmed case by case rather than assumed.

Domestic Transfers From Davao

The most common domestic routes are Davao to Manila and Davao to Cebu, usually for specialist referral. A dedicated air ambulance may be considered when a patient needs continuous monitoring or advanced medical support during the journey. A dedicated air ambulance may be considered when a patient needs continuous monitoring or advanced monitoring.

For stable patients, a medical escort on a scheduled flight may be appropriate. Patients who must lie flat may be considered for an airline stretcher: Philippine Airlines, for example, operates onboard stretcher service on domestic sectors between Manila and Cebu or Davao, subject to medical clearance, with the MEDIF submitted five to seven days before travel and an able-bodied escort required.

International Transfers From Davao

A medically configured aircraft can operate from Davao to regional and longer-haul destinations, subject to aircraft range and clearances. Starting the journey in Davao, rather than first moving the patient to Manila, reduces the number of transfers the patient goes through. Whether that is possible in a given case depends on the aircraft, the route and the permissions involved.

How The Transfer is Arranged

  1. Case intake: patient details, current hospital, destination and reason for transfer.
  2. Medical assessment by the medical team, with a call to the treating doctor.
  3. Receiving hospital confirms a bed and an accepting physician.
  4. Transport recommendation: air ambulance, airline stretcher or medical escort.
  5. Aircraft, crew and clearances, where a dedicated aircraft is used.
  6. Ground ambulance planning at both ends.
  7. Bedside pickup, flight and handover to the receiving hospital.

The full sequence is set out in our step-by-step guide to arranging an emergency air ambulance.

Documents

For domestic transfers: patient identification, the medical report and transfer summary, fit-to-fly documentation, and the receiving hospital's acceptance. For international transfers, add a valid passport, visas or entry requirements where applicable, passports for accompanying relatives, consent from the patient or next of kin, and insurance authorisation where an insurer is paying.

Cost Factors

A quotation depends on the origin and destination, distance and routing, the patient's condition and level of care required, the transport option, aircraft type, medical team composition, equipment and oxygen, ground ambulance at both ends, airport handling and clearances, and urgency. 

Returning OFWs Arriving in Davao

Workers repatriated from abroad often arrive through Davao rather than Manila. OWWA Region XI told the Philippine Information Agency that its airport assistance teams are available around the clock for arriving OFWs, including workers who are physically or mentally ill, and described providing transport home for repatriated workers. FAQs

Which airport is used for medical flights from Davao? Francisco Bangoy International Airport, also known as Davao International Airport, is the main departure point for the region.

Can a patient be collected from a hospital outside Davao City? Often yes. The route may combine a ground ambulance with the flight, or use an aircraft able to operate into a nearer regional airport where one is suitable and available.

Can a patient fly directly from Davao to another country? Subject to aircraft range, permissions and the medical assessment, yes. The alternative is a domestic leg to Manila first, which adds a transfer for the patient.

Is an airline stretcher available on domestic routes from Davao? Philippine Airlines lists onboard stretcher service on the Manila to Cebu and Davao sectors, subject to medical clearance and advance booking. Availability on a specific flight has to be confirmed with the airline.
